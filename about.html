About Inga

Our History

Indo German Alkaloids, the parent company of the INGA Group, was established in 1958 by Dr. Abraham Patani on completion of his Doctoral studies in Phytochemistry from the University of Mainz, Germany. With in-depth domain expertise in field of Alkaloids, the company began its manufacturing activities with a range of phytochemicals and fine chemicals.
