About Inga

Our History

Indo German Alkaloids, the parent company of the INGA Group, was established in 1958 by Dr. Abraham Patani on completion of his Doctoral studies in Phytochemistry from the University of Mainz, Germany. With in-depth domain expertise in field of Alkaloids, the company began its manufacturing activities with a range of phytochemicals and fine chemicals.

As a pioneer in alkaloid research, Indo German Alkaloids initiated the cultivation of plants required for the isolation of active principles at plantations in the northern, southern and western parts of India. With a vision to be one of the leading players in the niche phytochemical segment, Inga Laboratories P. Ltd. was established in 1974 for the manufacture of Finished dosage forms.
