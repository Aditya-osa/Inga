Marketing & Distribution

Our proven capability to research, manufacture and market high quality products is an endorsement of our ability to meet domestic and global standards. Inga distributes number of its speciality brands in the Indian sub-continent, Africa and South-East Asia, Latam and CIS.

With its aggressive marketing and efficient distribution, Inga Laboratories has carved a niche for itself in some therapeutic areas such as anti-migraine, anti-gout and systemic alkalizers.

The Company has a strong field force of over 250 field personnel who meet approximately 50,000 doctors. The distribution of Inga's products in India is facilitated through a wide network of over 600 distributors and stockists with 9 depots strategically located, reaching out to retail pharmacies.
